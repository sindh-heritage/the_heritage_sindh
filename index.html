04 — LISTEN TO THE SOUL✧

POETRY, WISDOM & MUSIC

Some stories
aren’t written.
They are sung.

For generations, Sindh’s poets and musicians have given voice to love, courage, humanity, and devotion.

Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai’s poetry lives on in Shah jo Risalo and the musical tradition of Shah jo Raag. Instruments such as the tamburo and yaktaro carry the spirit of folk expression.

An original ambient soundscape, not a traditional performance.

About this journey.

Created as an educational digital exhibition celebrating the heritage of Sindh. These seven chapters are an introduction to a diverse, living culture.

All scenes are original, stylized illustrations. The ancient city and architectural panorama are artistic interpretations, not exact archaeological reconstructions. The blue drainage line is illustrative, and the Ajrak printing sequence simplifies a much longer craft process. The optional audio is an original synthesized ambient composition, not a recording of Sindhi folk music.
